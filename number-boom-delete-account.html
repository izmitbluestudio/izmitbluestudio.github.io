Account and Data Deletion

Delete your Number Boom account and associated game data.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Number Boom on the device holding your account.
  2. Leave any ongoing match, then open Settings → Account and Privacy.
  3. Select Delete My Account and confirm permanent deletion.

This removes your account ID, nickname, score, wins, character appearance, friends, invitations and account-linked activity and reward records from our live database. The action is permanent. Rotating backups expire within fourteen days; independent advertising providers follow their own retention policies.

Request deletion outside the app

Email izmitbluestudio@gmail.com with the subject “Number Boom account deletion”. Include your nickname and, if available, the account ID shown in Settings → Account and Privacy. You can request account deletion or describe particular data you want removed. Do not send session tokens, passwords or other secret credentials.

We may ask for reasonable evidence of ownership so that another person cannot delete your account. We will respond with verification instructions or confirmation. This request method remains available if the app is uninstalled.
