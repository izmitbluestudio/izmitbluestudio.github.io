Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026. This policy applies to the Number Boom mobile game, published by izmitbluestudio.

Information used by the game

Choosing a nickname creates a persistent guest account on our game server. We store an account ID, nickname, authentication-token hash, character appearance, score, wins, friends, invitations, match participation, and first and last activity times. Daily account activity is used to calculate active-user statistics. Your device stores its session token and local preferences. We do not require your real name, email, password, contacts, microphone, camera, or GPS location.

While you play, the server processes movement, character actions, four-digit game codes, connection status and network addresses to run matches and protect the service. Other players can see your nickname, character, in-game actions, score and online presence where those features apply. Friends and invitations use in-game notifications.

Why we process information

We use game data to authenticate accounts, provide matchmaking and reconnection, display rankings and friends, deliver invitations, maintain scores and rewards, diagnose faults, calculate usage statistics and prevent abuse. Online account and gameplay data is necessary for online play.

Advertising and technical services

We use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Google's SDK may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Consent messages and ad privacy options are shown where required. Rewarded ads are optional. A reward ticket and completion status may be associated with your account to prevent duplicate rewards.

Unity technology and enabled diagnostics may process technical device, application, performance and crash information. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process access information. These providers process information under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity game player privacy policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

We do not sell your account records. Information sent to our game server uses encrypted TLS connections; advertising SDK transmissions use their providers' security mechanisms.

Retention and deletion

Account-linked game and usage records are retained while your account exists. Friend requests expire after seven days and lobby invitations after fifteen minutes. Short-lived connection and match records may also be processed for operation and troubleshooting.

You can request permanent deletion in the game through Settings → Account and Privacy → Delete My Account. Leave any ongoing match first. Deletion removes the account, nickname, scores, appearance, friends, invitations and linked activity and reward records from the live database. It cannot be undone. Local graphics and control preferences may remain on your device.

Rotating server backups can retain previous copies for up to fourteen days before being overwritten. Provider-side advertising or diagnostic data follows each provider's retention policies. Records required for a legal obligation or security incident may be retained only as necessary.

Account and data deletion instructions

Your choices and contact

You may use the available ad privacy controls, change device advertising-ID settings, decline optional rewarded ads, or delete your account. Number Boom is intended for ages 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child under 13 has provided account data.

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact izmitbluestudio@gmail.com. Include the game name and your nickname or account ID; do not send passwords or session tokens. We may ask for reasonable evidence of account ownership before taking action. We update this page when our practices change.
