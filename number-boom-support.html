Number Boom Go Support

Contact us

For game problems, privacy questions or account deletion help, email izmitbluestudio@gmail.com. You may write in your preferred language.

Include your game version, device model, operating system, nickname and a description of the problem. Screenshots are helpful. Never send passwords, authentication tokens or payment details.

Connection and matchmaking

An internet connection is required. If a match does not connect, check your connection and restart the game. Tell us when the error occurred and include its message when contacting support.

Guest access and saved progress

The game does not require an email address or password. Your device holds access to your guest profile. Deleting the app or its data may remove that access. Account recovery is currently unavailable.

Voice chat and player reports

Voice listening and microphone transmission have separate controls. Use the in-game mute, block and report options for inappropriate behaviour. Do not share personal information in voice chat.

Rewarded advertisements

Rewarded ads are optional and depend on availability. Complete the advertisement to qualify for its reward. If something goes wrong, include the approximate time and error message in your support request.

Privacy and deletion

Read our Privacy Policy and account deletion instructions. You can request deletion through the game's Account and Privacy settings. Leave an ongoing match first.
